Back to the Future: The 2020 Car Key Milestone

Published on June 23, 2020

It's been a long time coming, but yesterday Apple announced support for NFC-based digital car keys at WWDC 2020.

Ten years ago, in 2010, I predicted that within three years I would be able to leave my house with only my phone. In 2015, I checked in to see where things stood.

Now that's finally becoming a reality, with Apple Pay, digital car keys, sat nav (maps), and of course, the phone itself consolidating into a single device.

So, what's next? Well, that's easy: in ten years from now, I won't have to leave my house at all (if I don't want to)!
